Sounds and music of the R(ust)PG2D starter library and example project

Every file here was made for R(ust)PG2D, synthesized by small programs (no samples, no
recordings), and is released with the project under the MIT License (see the project's
LICENSE). Games made with R(ust)PG2D may use them freely.

  music_*      scripts/sounds/music (a small synthesizer; the track's name in brackets)
  amb_*, step_*, ui_*, magic_*, coin, quest_done, page
               scripts/sounds/make_sounds.py
  fanfare_*    synthesized for R(ust)PG2D 0.26 (scripts not kept)
  the other effects
               the example generator of R(ust)PG2D 0.10 (removed since)
